KidoPuzzle Support

Help with puzzles, settings and your subscription.

Contact

Email mgokhan.yavuz@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I open Settings?

Tap the gear button on the home screen and answer the short adult question (the parental gate). There you can change the language, turn music, sound effects and haptics on or off, restore purchases and reset progress.

How do I make puzzles easier or harder?

Before a puzzle starts, pick the number of stars on the left (4, 6, 9, 12 or 16 pieces) and the cut style on the right (classic, wavy or square). Each puzzle has a few hints; tap the light bulb to show where a piece goes.

There's no sound

  • Check that Music and Sound Effects are on in KidoPuzzle's Settings.
  • Turn up the device volume and make sure the Ring/Silent switch or Focus mode isn't muting media.

The app is in the wrong language

KidoPuzzle follows your device language by default. To choose another one, open Settings in the app and pick a language.

Subscription

  • Free puzzles: the first puzzles in every theme are free. KidoPuzzle Pro unlocks all puzzles and new themes.
  • Manage or cancel: Settings app > your name > Subscriptions > KidoPuzzle. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
  • Restore a purchase: in KidoPuzzle, open Settings > Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA.

Deleting your data

Progress and preferences are stored only on your device. Use Settings > Reset Progress, or delete the app to remove everything. See the Privacy Policy for details.
