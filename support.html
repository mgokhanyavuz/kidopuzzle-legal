Help with puzzles, coloring, settings and your subscription.

Contact

How do I open Settings?

Tap the gear button at the top right of the puzzle or coloring home screen and answer the short adult question (the parental gate). There you can change the language, turn music, sound effects and haptics on or off, restore purchases and reset puzzle progress.

How do I make puzzles easier or harder?

Before a puzzle starts, pick the number of stars on the left (4, 6, 9, 12 or 16 pieces) and the cut style on the right (classic, wavy or square). Each puzzle has a few hints; tap the light bulb to show where a piece goes.

How does coloring work?

Choose Coloring on the first screen, pick a theme at the top and tap a page. Pick a tool on the left and a color on the right: the bucket fills an area with one tap, and the brushes never go outside the lines. Paintings are saved automatically. The arrow button undoes the last step and the trash button clears the page.

How do I save a painting to Photos?

On the coloring screen, tap the pink save button and answer the adult question. The first time, iOS asks for permission to add photos. If saving fails, open the Settings app > KidoPlay > Photos and allow "Add Photos Only". The yellow camera button is just for fun: it plays a shutter sound and does not take or save a real photo.

There's no sound

  • Check that Music and Sound Effects are on in KidoPlay's Settings.
  • Turn up the device volume and make sure the Ring/Silent switch or Focus mode isn't muting media.

The app is in the wrong language

KidoPlay follows your device language by default. To choose another one, open Settings in the app and pick a language.

Subscription

  • Free content: the first puzzle and the first coloring page in every theme are free. KidoPlay Pro unlocks all puzzles, all coloring pages and new themes with one subscription.
  • Manage or cancel: Settings app > your name > Subscriptions > KidoPlay. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
  • Restore a purchase: in KidoPlay, open Settings > Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA.

Deleting your data

Progress, paintings and preferences are stored only on your device. Use Settings > Reset Progress for puzzle stars, the trash button on a coloring page to clear that painting, or delete the app to remove everything. See the Privacy Policy for details.
