Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

KidoPuzzle ("we", "our", "the app") is a jigsaw puzzle game for young children aged 2 to 6. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and your rights under COPPA, GDPR, CCPA and other applicable laws. The app is designed for children and is built so that it does not collect personal information from them.

Data We Collect

KidoPuzzle does not require an account and never asks a child for a name, photo, email address, location or any other personal information. The following stays on your device only:

  • Puzzle progress: stars earned and favorite themes
  • The age range a parent picks during setup (used only to suggest a starting difficulty)
  • App preferences: language, music, sound effects, haptics, piece count and cut style

KidoPuzzle does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or social features, and it contains no links to the web or other apps outside the parental gate.

Data Storage

The data above is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. It is not uploaded to our servers, and we do not operate servers that receive it. Deleting the app deletes this data, and you can also reset progress in the app's Settings. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include the app's data in those backups according to your device settings.

Parental Gate

Settings, purchases and any external links are protected by a parental gate (a simple adult check), so children cannot reach them on their own.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store processes subscription payments. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status for the adult who buys KidoPuzzle Pro. To validate purchases it receives an anonymous app user ID, transaction and subscription details, and basic device and app information (such as OS version, app version, locale and IP address). It is not used to identify anyone, for advertising or for tracking. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We Do Not Sell or Track

KidoPuzzle shows no ads, does not track users across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell or share personal information.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Gameplay data never leaves the device. If you believe information about your child has been collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Because gameplay data never leaves your device, you can reset it in the app or remove it completely by deleting the app. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, you can ask us to access, correct or delete them by contacting us below. We process purchase data to provide the subscription you requested (performance of a contract).

Your Rights (CCPA / California)

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA. You may request access to or deletion of purchase records by contacting us below, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Data Retention

Data on your device is kept until you reset it or delete the app. RevenueCat retains transaction records for as long as needed to manage the subscription and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
